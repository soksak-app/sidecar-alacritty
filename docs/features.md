- [ ] S74 — P0: Closing a session ends the jobs of its shell. Input: `sleep 1000 &` in an interactive zsh, then `close`. Behavior, read from the code: `close` sends SIGKILL to the shell and to its process group, so the shell cannot pass a hangup to a job of its own group and the job survives, against `persistent-services.md`, which completes a close only after the managed jobs are terminated. Expected: `close` sends SIGHUP to the child and waits up to 250 ms for the reaper; a child that remains and the process group then receive SIGKILL. The wait has a timer because a program that ignores SIGHUP produces no event. The `kill` of `portable-pty` has this form and is not used, because its `try_wait` reaps and the reaper alone reaps here. A job started with `nohup` or `disown` keeps running.
